# MTJR

Merchant-first commerce platform. Digitize real-world (offline/outdoor) merchants at ground
level, put a well-designed marketplace on top that connects buyers and sellers, and use the
resulting demand data to eventually decide what to manufacture.

Name: **MTJR** — reads as متجر (*matjar*, "store") and تاجر (*tajir*, "merchant").

> Status: **concept / planning only.** No code yet. Private by design — the demand-intelligence
> thesis is the differentiator.

## The one-paragraph pitch

B2B players (Cartona, MaxAB-Wasoko) digitize retailers for wholesale; consumer players
(Amazon.eg, Noon) don't digitize small merchants at all. MTJR sits in the gap: consumer-facing
discovery over hand-digitized local merchants, with **demand intelligence as the stated
product** — what people need, why, in what bundles, and what they expect — building toward
consumer-to-manufacturer (C2M) production, the model Shein/Temu proved, applied bottom-up from
local supply.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **Asset-light.** No owned warehouses, no delivery fleet, no lending — every peer that went
  asset-heavy shut down.
- **Depth over breadth.** One neighborhood, one category, real density — not a shallow national
  map.
- **Sequencing discipline.** Payments, logistics, credit, government registration, multi-city,
  manufacturing — each is a company-killer at the wrong time.
